# Privacy Policy

Effective 16 November 2026.

Short version: this website has no accounts, no forms, no cookies and no analytics, and the software doesn't send anything to us.

## This website

The site is a set of static pages. It does not use cookies, tracking pixels, analytics or advertising, and it does not ask you for any personal information.

Loading a page does involve these services, which may receive your IP address and browser details as part of normal web requests:

| Service | Why | Their policy |
|---|---|---|
| Netlify | Hosts the site and may keep short-lived server logs for security and reliability. | [Netlify privacy](https://www.netlify.com/privacy/) |
| Google Fonts | Serves the Geist and Geist Mono fonts. | [Google privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) |
| jsDelivr | Serves the Paper Shaders library used for the hero background. | [jsDelivr privacy](https://www.jsdelivr.com/terms/privacy-policy-jsdelivr-net) |

Links to other websites, such as GitHub, follow those websites' own policies.

## The software

The After Effects × Claude MCP server runs on your computer. It has no telemetry and does not send data to the authors.

- It writes job and result files to a local folder on your computer (on macOS, `~/Library/Application Support/AE Claude Bridge`) and renders frames there.
- It talks to After Effects on the same computer through AppleScript, or the optional Claude Bridge panel.
- It loads your style profile from a file on your computer.

What you type into Claude, and what Claude reads back from the server (for example layer names or rendered frames), goes through your Claude app to Anthropic and is handled under [Anthropic's privacy policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy), not this one.

## Children

This website and software are not directed at children and collect no information from anyone.
